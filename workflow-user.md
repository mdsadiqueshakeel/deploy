# MLM System User Guide

Welcome to the MLM (Multi-Level Marketing) System! This guide will help you understand how to use the platform, even if you have no technical background.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Registering an Account
- Go to the registration page (ask your sponsor for the link or use a referral link).
- Fill in your name, email, password, and the referral code (if you have one).
- Click **Register**. You will receive a confirmation email if required.

### Logging In
- Go to the login page.
- Enter your email and password.
- Click **Login** to access your dashboard.

---

## 2. Joining with a Referral Link
- If you received a referral link from someone, click the link to open the registration page.
- The referral code will be filled in automatically.
- Complete the registration form and submit.
- You will be placed in the binary tree under your sponsor.

---

## 3. Your Dashboard
- After logging in, you will see your dashboard.
- The sidebar lets you navigate to different sections: Dashboard, Products, Business, Wallet, Status, Rank & Rewards, Support, and Profile.
- Your profile info and referral codes are shown on your dashboard and profile page.

---

## 4. Viewing and Updating Your Profile
- Click **Profile** in the sidebar.
- View your personal details, bank details, and referral codes.
- To update your info, click **Edit Profile**, make changes, and click **Update Profile**.
- You can also change your password and upload or remove your avatar.

---

## 5. Business Volume
- Click **Business** in the sidebar.
- Here you can:
  - **Add Business Volume**: Enter the amount and submit to add your business volume.
  - **View Business Volume**: See your personal volume and the volume in your left and right teams.

---

## 6. Income
- Your income is calculated based on your business volume and your team's performance.
- Types of income:
  - **Matching Income**: Earned when both your left and right teams have matching business volume.
  - **Level Income**: Earned from the activity of users in your referral levels (up to 30 levels).
- You can view your income stats on the dashboard or in the income section (if available).

---

## 7. Using Referral Codes
- Your profile shows your **Left Referral Code** and **Right Referral Code**.
- Share these codes or links with others to invite them to your left or right team.
- When someone registers with your code, they join your team and help you grow your business volume.

---

## 8. Support
- If you need help, click **Support** in the sidebar.
- You can find FAQs or contact support for assistance.

---

## 9. Logging Out
- Click the **Logout** button at the bottom of the sidebar to safely log out of your account.

---

## 10. Tips
- Keep your profile and bank details up to date to ensure smooth payouts.
- Regularly check your business volume and income stats to track your progress.
- Use your referral codes to grow your network and increase your earnings.

---

**Enjoy building your network and growing your income with the MLM System!**
